Spelling it Out

A collaborative mixed-media poster project celebrating
Lamar Dodd School of Art's 100th birthday

It was important to my classmates and I to embrace design
in as many forms as possible. Exploring 3D design, we each
made a unique paper letter using different techniques. These
would be featured in our posters to spell out our central phrase:
“Celebrate the Centennial.” My letter L was made with over 100
individual origami stacking elements.

A place like Lamar Dodd truly thrives because of the
people in it. To honor our community's creativity & support
and our opportunity to benefit from it by learning here, we
decided each designer must be featured in their poster.

After collaborating to take our photos, we began
bringing the posters to life digitally. Legibility was an
incredibly important factor: Together, we critiqued
and refined our work to create a readable final picture.
I offered insight on others’ posters and adapted my own
design many times to best embody the spirit of the Dodd.

The result...?

A grand birthday party for the Dodd!

These posters will remain in the hallway of the Dodd until
Spring of 2027. They celebrate and create awareness for the
Dodd's centennial anniversary, but they also celebrate graphic
design and all of the other wonderful programs the Dodd offers.

This project gave me the opportunity to learn so much about
photography, photoshop, and print design. Throughout every
step of my design process, I overcame challenges in formatting
or execution that ultimately helped me grow as a designer.
